Who I am

Technology skills backed by real-world experience.

My route into technology has combined university projects, independent learning, customer-facing work and practical manufacturing experience.

I graduated from the University of Kent with a degree in Business Information Technology. The course allowed me to explore software development, web technologies, databases, algorithms, project management, entrepreneurship and the way technology supports organisations.

What interests me most is using technology to solve a genuine problem. I enjoy taking an idea, breaking it into manageable parts and gradually turning it into something that works, whether that is a website, an application, a dashboard, a small game or a workflow improvement.

My employment history has also given me experience in manufacturing, hospitality, leisure and retail. Those roles helped me develop communication, reliability, teamwork, customer awareness and the ability to remain calm in busy environments.

I am especially interested in roles involving IT support, technical operations, hardware, front-end development and user experience. I enjoy learning new systems and I am comfortable asking questions, investigating problems and working towards a practical answer.
