My story

I like making technology feel useful, not complicated.

Studying Business Information Technology gave me the chance to learn both how digital products are built and why organisations invest in them. That combination suits the way I think: I enjoy the technical detail, but I always want to understand the purpose behind it.

At university I worked across web development, Java, databases, cloud concepts, algorithms, project management, and user experience. Projects let me put those areas together—from a Firebase-backed sports club app to a creative coding tool and a full-stack appointment system.

What real-world work added

My roles in manufacturing, hospitality, leisure, and retail taught me things code alone cannot: how to stay dependable under pressure, communicate with different people, maintain quality, and contribute to a team when the pace is high.

Those experiences have made me more commercially aware and more comfortable taking responsibility. I bring that same mindset to technical work—be clear, be thorough, and keep moving the task forward.

Where I’m heading

I’m looking for a graduate or junior opportunity in IT, software, digital operations, or a related area where I can keep learning while contributing from day one. I’m particularly drawn to roles that mix problem-solving, people, and practical delivery.
